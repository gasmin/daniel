For your nineteenth birthday — September 8

Daniel

For your birthday, I tried to put our journey into words — the places, the music, the warmth, and all the little moments that became ours. I hope you like it.

June

Our beginning

Mountains, late trains, a chocolate croissant.
It began in June— with mountains, late trains, a chocolate croissant, and the feeling that "with you it's better."
A World Alone – Lorde
Home

Our hot summer nights

The Danube, still summer evenings, and home appearing in ordinary places.
We found romance in wrong turns, in Ulm beside the Danube, in summer evenings that made the whole world feel briefly still. Then came the nights of warmth, laughter, desire— until ordinary places began to feel like home, because you were there. "Home's not home unless you're there."
Michelle Pfeiffer – Ethel Cain
Belonging

Our risk

Sunlight, language, belonging, and choosing love again.
You carry Spain with you: its sunlight, its language, the place that shaped you and will always be part of where you belong. And somewhere between our songs and our silences, love became a risk— one I would take again. "You're the risk, I'm gonna take it."
Risk – Gracie Abrams
Nineteen

Our life ahead

New roads, new cities, and a life still opening in front of you.
You are nineteen now, with your whole life ahead of you, new roads, new cities, and so much still waiting. "Got your whole life ahead of you, you're only 19."
teenage dream – Olivia Rodrigo
Colors

Our beauty

Softness, humour, curiosity, and moments stitched together with love.
I hope you keep your softness, your humour, your curiosity, and the beautiful chaos that makes you entirely yourself. Because all these little moments— the mountains, the river, the late trains, the hospital room, the songs and the nights— became something I will carry. Not perfect, but full of colour, stitched together with love. "Coat of many colors."
Coat of Many Colors – Dolly Parton
Daniel

Our love

A last wish, and a love carried into what comes next.
So wherever life takes you, whether close or far away, remember that you were loved in the beginning, in the uncertainty, and in everything still to come. Happy birthday, Daniel. And wherever you go: "I will always love you."
I Will Always Love You – Dolly Parton
